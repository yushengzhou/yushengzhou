# ADR-0006: Leave badges, stats, and similar widgets out of the profile

- Date: 2026-11-25
- Status: accepted

## Context

GitHub profile READMEs often add skill badges, stats cards, trophies, visitor counters, star history, auto-follow, a phone number, and referees. On 2026-11-25 the owner left that set out of this profile. The demand card records the same boundary.

## Decision

Those items are out of scope: badges, stats, trophies, counters, star history, auto-follow, phone, and referees. The profile shows the typing introduction, the research-interests graph, the contribution snake, and three contact links (personal site, ORCID, and the public email).

## Rationale

The owner asked for a short academic page. The widgets above are a different page. The contact block is the site, the ORCID, and the email already published on the profile.

## Consequences

- Putting any of those widgets on the profile is a new decision, not a small edit under this one.
- The contact section stays at three links. It does not grow a phone number or a referee list.
